EXECUTIVE FAQ

Frequently Asked Cyber Liability Questions

Does Commercial General Liability (CGL) cover ransomware payments?

No. Standard CGL policies contain explicit electronic data exclusions. Only dedicated Cyber Liability policies reimburse extortion payments, ransom negotiations, and forensic restoration.

Are ransomware payments legal to pay?

Yes, provided the threat actor syndicate is not listed on the US Treasury OFAC sanctions registry. Our licensed extortion negotiators perform real-time OFAC compliance checks prior to authorization.

What happens if a dependent cloud vendor (AWS/Azure) goes down?

Our Cyber Business Interruption policies include Dependent System Outage endorsements, covering your business profit losses when third-party cloud vendors suffer cyber downtime.

How fast can CyberGuard bind a policy?

For SMBs and mid-market firms with standard security controls, policy binding and Certificate of Insurance (COI) issuance takes less than 24 hours.
